# Study Notes: Density

## What Density Is

**Density** — mass present in a certain volume
**Mass** — the amount of matter in a body
**Volume** — the space occupied by a body

- Density means how much mass is present in a certain volume.
- Formula: density = mass / volume
- Mass is the amount of matter in a body.
- Volume is the space occupied by a body.

## Units of Density

**SI unit** — kilograms per cubic metre (kg/m³)
**g/cm³** — grams per cubic centimetre, also commonly used
**Water** — density of about 1000 kg/m³ or 1 g/cm³

- The SI unit of density is kg per cubic metre (kg/m³).
- g/cm³ is also commonly used.
- The density of water is about 1000 kg/m³, or 1 g/cm³.

## How Mass and Volume Affect Density

**Greater density** — more mass in the same volume
**Smaller density** — the same mass spread over a greater volume
**Identifying materials** — density can help identify what a material is

- If the same volume has more mass, the density is greater.
- If the same mass occupies a greater volume, the density becomes smaller.
- Density can help identify materials.

## Review Questions

1. Explain in your own words what density is, and write the formula that relates it to mass and volume.
2. Two objects have the same mass, but one occupies a greater volume. Which has the greater density, and why?
3. State the SI unit of density and the density of water in both kg/m³ and g/cm³. How could density help you identify a material?
